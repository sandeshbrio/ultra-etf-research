Trading Lens

  • The NVDA board story is a live driver, not a priced print — the rule-8 confirmation check at ~10:30 AM is the discriminator. SOXL's gap is unwinding and deepening through the morning (−3.9% at 5:47 AM → −10% at 8:15 AM) while the driver worsens; that is the live-driver shape, the opposite of Wednesday's single-print CoreWeave gap that faded at the check. A SOXS/SQQQ gap that is still extending at the check with the board story unresolved (or worsening) is the continuation shape; a gap fading toward Wednesday's close by the check is the stand-aside. Do not chase the open either way.
  • The 10-year at 5% is the day's second live test, and it lands mid-session. Cash Treasuries reopen today after the Veterans Day closure, and the $25B 30-year auction at 1:00 PM is the first real demand test since Tuesday's hot CPI and soft 10-year auction. A weak auction pushing the long end through 5% would stack a rates headwind on top of the NVDA fade — nothing leveraged carries through that window unhedged by the check.
  • Wednesday's +17.8% SOXL close is now the eleventh documented winning-side miss — and the stand-aside still dodged a bigger loser on the other side. The ledger's lesson from Wednesday stands: the check judges the driver, not the close, and a gap that extends after the window does not license chasing the next one. Today the pair has flipped — the same discipline now guards the inverse side, where SOXS is +10% premarket on a story that could resolve (board backs Huang) as fast as it broke.
  • Friday's PPI is the week's second binary — stay flat into it, same as Tuesday. A hot PPI (+0.4% headline / +0.3% core expected) on top of a hot CPI hardens the 50 bp talk; a soft one is the only print that can cut December-hike odds. Between now and then, the book's job is to trade today's reaction, not to hold a view — and nothing leveraged carries into Friday's 8:30 AM print.
