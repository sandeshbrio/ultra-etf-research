  1. The Nvidia story has moved from shock to stabilization attempt — and the tape is not yet convinced. NVDA is +~2% premarket after Thursday's −4.34% close, with 1,600+ employees signing a petition for Huang's return and an internal poll showing 97% support, while Bernstein slashed its target to $200 (from $400) and Goldman kept Buy but cut to $220 (from $300). Cathie Wood backed the engineering bench ("beyond the Jensen show"). The key level is Thursday's $148.80 low. Nvidia reports earnings Tue Nov 17 — the first print of the post-Huang company, guided to $108.0B revenue. Longbridge, TipRanks, World Money Roadmap
  2. The 10-year above 5% is now the tape's co-driver, and it is louder than the Nvidia story for the broad market. Thursday's +11–13 bp move, the tailed 30-year auction, and a December hike priced as near-certain (~87–91% for 25 bp) mean PPI lands on a market already pricing tighter policy. A hot PPI hardens the 50 bp talk; a soft one is the only print that can cut hike odds. The 5% line on the 10-year is the level to watch through the session. PreMarket Daily, Saxo options brief
  3. The AI-infrastructure earnings ballot split — and the split is the information. Cisco beat and raised (adj. EPS $1.40 vs $1.33, revenue $18.5B +24%, FY guide lifted to $74.8–75.8B, hyperscaler AI revenue guided to $9B in FY27) — then faded to −0.39% after hours and −2%+ premarket. Two clean beats, two different survivals: the market is buying equipment demand and doubting networking follow-through, or simply refusing to chase any AI print into PPI. Either way, the "beat = rally" reflex is broken this week. Narrative Flow Intel, TrendyVest
  4. Asia's chip rebound faded by the afternoon — the relief rally has no follow-through yet. The KOSPI opened +2.05% and touched 5,504, then closed −0.98%; the Nikkei gave back most of a +1.64% midday gain to close +0.53%; only the Taiex held its +1.35%. A relief bounce that cannot hold into the close is a fade, not a base — and it leaves SOXL/SOXS pointed at PPI with no directional cushion from overnight. MLQ, The Edge Asia wrap
