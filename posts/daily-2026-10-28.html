With a hold this expected, the statement and 2:30 PM Q&A — especially any December signal — move markets more than the decision itself. A dovish tone sparks a rally; hawkish tightening talk triggers a sell-off. Invezz 5 things, OptionsPro pre-market outlook, Risk Reversal
  3. Hyperscaler capex is tonight's verdict on the AI trade (high stakes). Microsoft and Alphabet report after the close; the question is not just cloud growth but whether accelerating AI capex converts to revenue. Options price ~5–6% moves in each name.
