Thursday the story landed: Huang resigned as president, CEO and board member effective immediately, no reason given in the 8-K. NVDA opened nearly 12% lower, touched $148.80, and recovered to close $166.19 (−4.34%) on ~743M shares, while the succession-trade rivals rose in a block (AMD +5.86%, Broadcom +5.54%). CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO; the board hired an outside search firm. The 10-year closed ~5.10% after the weakest $25B 30-year auction since the summer, and gold printed a 13-month low. Friday's October PPI was the week's second inflation leg — headline +0.3% m/m (below the +0.4% forecast) but +6.5% y/y, the fastest since November 2022; core +0.3% m/m and +5.0% y/y, the highest since January 2023. Stocks opened higher on the softer monthly number, then faded as chips gave back Thursday's relief (SOX −3.43%, AMD −8.75%, NVDA −2%+ to ~$162.85, −12.75% on the week) and the 10-year closed the week at 5.15%. The week's net: every major index higher, the losing streak snapped — and the tape still closed the week staring at a 5.15% 10-year and a December hike priced as near-certain.
