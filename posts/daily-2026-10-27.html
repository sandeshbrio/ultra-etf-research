Sentiment Drivers

  1. Anthropic's IPO filing is still the tape's tailwind (bullish tech). Monday's rally was led by the Claude maker's public S-1 (Nasdaq ticker "ANT"), with strong revenue growth in the filing easing fears the AI boom is slowing — and Tuesday's Asian session extended it (KOSPI +3%+, Nikkei +2%). "The decision to proceed with an IPO filing amid recent volatility in the AI trade may itself be seen as a vote of confidence," per Tiger Brokers' James Ooi. Reuters via WNFL
  2. The AI supply chain is getting paid, even as the hyperscalers dilute (bullish chips, mixed mega-cap). Celestica surged ~13–15% premarket on a beat on every line plus a 2027 forecast above Street estimates; Nvidia is taking a $3B chunk of Meta's upsized $97.5B equity raise. The read: orders for the boxes, racks and cooling are still arriving, even as funding questions swirl around the renters of compute. Samuel & Co morning brief, Invezz premarket
  3. Leveraged-ETF inflows are at a record pace — a contrarian flag (caution). The 10 largest US leveraged equity ETFs have drawn $12B+ in the past month ($19B over five months); SOXL alone took in $9B+ in two weeks, its largest ever, and TQQQ $1.3B+, its second-largest two-week intake of 2026 — even as the funds' combined market cap fell ~$20B. Retail is piling into leverage into the week's biggest event window. That flow supports the bounce but also means a Wednesday/Thursday disappointment has a crowded exit. Stocktwits/Kobeissi
  4. Oil easing but still the inflation tail (neutral-to-bearish for the hold narrative). Brent ~$110 after Monday's $115.81 settle, with Saudi, Turkish and Pakistani officials in talks with Tehran — but Houthi attacks continue and Washington hints at more strikes. Markets price ~90% odds of a Fed hold Wednesday but ~73% odds of a December hike, with oil above $100 keeping that hike path alive. A re-spike re-arms the whole risk-off chain into Thursday's PCE. NordFX Market Pulse, Reuters via SRN
  5. Consumer earnings are beating — a quiet positive under the AI noise (bullish consumer). UPS beat ($1.68 vs $1.62 expected, revenue $22.34B vs $22.28B) and lifted its full-year outlook; Coca-Cola beat on strong global demand; Royal Caribbean beat with strong 2027 bookings; PayPal beat. The consumer is holding up into the holiday season — which is exactly what today's 10:00 AM confidence print will test. Barron's Daily, WSNext UPS report

Trading Lens

  • SOXL/SOXS: ride the momentum, but respect the two-day vertical. SOXL +8.3% Monday plus +4.0% premarket is a ~12% two-day move on top of record inflows. The trend is unambiguously up this morning and the Asian chip tape backs it — but 3x ETFs that vertical rarely go a third day without a shakeout. If the gap holds through the first 30 minutes, SOXS stays a squeeze; if SOXL fades back under Monday's $90.50 close, the gap-fill trade (SOXS long) opens fast. Size for a round-trip, not a trend day.
  • TQQQ/SQQQ: reclaim attempt, not a reclaim. TQQQ $69.23 premarket has cleared the $67.30 bear-invalidation level, but the S&P still sits ~1.8% below its Oct 6 high and below its 50-day. SQQQ is the crowded, bleeding side this morning — only tradeable on a failed gap or a hot 10:00 AM confidence print, not into a +1.2% premarket bounce.
  • TNA/TZA: today's data is the trigger. Small caps lagged Monday (+0.38% vs SOX +2.82%), so TNA is not the momentum vehicle this morning — it's the data-reaction vehicle. Soft confidence/new-home-sales → TNA bounce candidate; hot prints → TZA, as rate-hike odds re-price. Don't pre-commit before 10:00 AM.
  • FAS: wait for Visa AMC. Banks are soft in Europe and the sector's direction this week runs through card spending (Visa tonight, Mastercard Thursday BMO), not through this morning's industrial/consumer beats. FAS is a post-close setup, not a premarket trade.

The job today is the same as Monday's, one rung higher: watch whether the SOXL/TQQQ gaps survive the 8:30 durable-goods and 10:00 confidence prints — that sets the entry levels for Wednesday's FOMC + hyperscaler gauntlet.
