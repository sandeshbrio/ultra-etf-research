Sentiment Drivers

  1. CPI is the day's binary — and Waller raised the stakes overnight. Consensus: headline +0.3% m/m (Sept +0.6%), core +0.2% (Sept +0.3%). There will be no official year-over-year rate — the 2025 shutdown left the BLS without October 2025 prices, so traders judge the print on the monthly numbers, core first (economists' own y/y estimates: ~3.9% headline, ~2.7–2.8% core). Fed Governor Waller said in London this morning he backs a 25 bp December hike even if inflation eases, and would consider a 50 bp move if core prints 0.3% or higher — the consensus +0.2% sits just below his trigger. December-hike odds are ~80–85%. Bank of America: if the forecast is right, the print effectively locks in another quarter-point hike absent a soft 0.1% reading. This is the last CPI before the Dec 8–9 FOMC, and the November CPI lands Dec 10 — a day after the decision. TradingNerve, World Money Roadmap, Binance/CNBC, Political.org/Reuters
  2. Monday's rebound was broad and real — but it was a relief rally, not a reclaim. All 11 sectors rose, the SOX surged 6.85%, the VIX fell by more than a fifth, and Asia followed through overnight with the KOSPI holding its +4.4% this time. Against that: the S&P is still ~2.7% below its Oct 6 record area, QQQ remains well off its highs after a −15.7% week, and the rally's fuel was an oil slide, not a fundamentals reset. A cool CPI extends it; a hot one hands the tape straight back to the inverse side.
  3. The Nvidia probe is contained — for now — but the legal timeline runs past the Nov 17 earnings. NVDA is ~+2.1% premarket after Monday's −4.27%, the only SOX decliner on the market's best day since April. The board says it has "no reason to believe" national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, and Huang remains CEO. The overhang: the Megaspeed smuggling probe has reportedly moved from Commerce to DOJ, and three House committee chairmen want a closed-door briefing with Huang on Nov 18. If the probe widens, the chip tape re-opens last Thursday's wound. AI Market Signal, Calculated Degens
  4. Oil's slide flips the signal from geopolitical to demand. Brent below $92 and WTI ~$86 after a ~6% Monday collapse, on Iran de-escalation hopes. Energy was last week's worst sector despite the Hormuz headlines; a further slide confirms the slowdown fear behind the equity rout, while a stabilization removes one of the market's few remaining tailwinds. Gold's cooling (−0.3%) says the haven trade is unwinding with it. XTB, Futures Sense India

Trading Lens

  • CPI is 13 minutes away — the book stays flat into the print, by rule. Trade the reaction, never the print. The trigger map is unusually clean: core ≥0.3% = Waller's 50 bp threat, yields spike, SQQQ/SPXU/TZA engage fast; core +0.2% = priced-in, likely a muted continuation of Monday's bounce; core ≤0.1% = relief, TQQQ/SOXL continuation with room to run. Wait for the first 15 minutes of cash trading to confirm direction before sizing anything.
  • Do not chase Monday's gaps in either direction. SOXL +22.25% and TQQQ +10.20% in a single session are extension, not entry — and this morning's flat-to-split futures say the tape knows it. A long continuation needs SOXL/TQQQ to hold Monday's range through the CPI reaction and Nvidia to stabilize; a short continuation needs a hot core print to break Monday's lows. Either way, size down: this pair moved ~40% in two sessions last week.
  • TNA is the CPI tell. Russell +3.15% Monday was the best index, TNA +9.62% the strongest liquid watchlist name. Small caps are the most rate-sensitive leg — a cool print is their best-case setup (TNA continuation), a hot one breaks them first (TZA). Watch whether TNA holds Monday's range after 8:30 AM; if it does, the bounce is broadening past chips.
  • Two volatility windows, not one. The 1:00 PM $42B 10-year auction is the afternoon's second event — a weak auction after a hot CPI print would compound the rates move, and Fed speakers (Logan 9:35 AM, Bowman/Schmid 1:30 PM, Bowman again 6:30 PM) can add noise all day. CoreWeave after the close is the AI-cloud read that matters for Wednesday's SOXL open — flat into it too, unless the CPI reaction gives a clean rule-8 continuation setup first.
