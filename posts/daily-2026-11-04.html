Sentiment Drivers

  1. AMD's print is the real story — a beat that actually held. Q3 revenue $13.4B (+45% YoY, vs ~$13.09B expected), adjusted EPS $2.09 (vs ~$1.95), data-center revenue $8.55B (+97% YoY, now 64% of sales). Q4 guidance ~$17B (±3%, vs ~$16.23B expected) and, critically, CEO Lisa Su's 2027 outlook: total revenue growth of more than 80%, with data center ~80% of sales. Shares +6–7% premarket, Nvidia +1.2–1.4%, and Goldman reiterated Buy with a $700 target. After Apple and Palantir fell on beats last week, AMD is the first mega-AI name to be rewarded for one — that resets the grading curve for tonight's Qualcomm and Arm prints. Caveats worth holding: Q4 gross margin guides down to ~55% from 56%, client/gaming revenue falls 20%+ sequentially, and free cash flow dropped to $554M. TipRanks, Invezz, Earnings Whispers
  2. The Democratic sweep is a two-way headline, not a direction. The initial read — tighter AI regulation, slower data-center spending, tech selloff — reversed within hours into a gridlock-is-good rebound. Both moves were real money at different hours of the night, which means the political premium is now embedded in both directions: any concrete policy headline (AI regulation, budget, tariff action) can re-ignite either leg intraday. Trade the price action, not the narrative. Kitco/Reuters, Deutsche Bank via FXStreet
  3. The overnight round trip is a warning about premarket signals. Nasdaq-100 futures went from −2.4% to +0.97% before the open; SOXL went from −8% overnight to −3.87% at 8:14 AM. Premarket extremes this morning are already stale by the time you read them — the 9:30 AM open, not the 8:00 AM quote, decides whether the rebound is real. Size accordingly and let the first 15 minutes confirm.
  4. Hot JOLTS reframes Friday's payrolls. Tuesday's 7.52M openings (vs ~7.15M expected) says the labor market is firmer than the rate-cut case assumed. Today's ADP (~65–70K expected) and 10:00 AM ISM Services (with its prices index ~75 expected) are the next two reads; a hot pair re-arms the December-hike case and undercuts the rebound — the exact setup SPXU/TZA would feed on. Edgyx, TheStreet Pro
  5. Oil's retreat removes one volatility leg. Brent −2.3% to ~$105.70 as the Iran-strike headlines cooled. Energy is no longer the session's hidden gap risk — but the round trip (an ~8% plunge Monday, a rebound Tuesday, a retreat today) shows how headline-driven it remains; don't chase energy on a single headline either way.
