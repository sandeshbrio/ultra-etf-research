Sentiment Drivers

  1. Anthropic is the day's main event — the largest IPO on record. Anthropic priced at $83 a share, above its $70–$78 marketed range, upsized to ~980 million shares, raising ~$81.8 billion and valuing the company near $1.5 trillion. It begins trading on Nasdaq today under ticker ANT. The pricing alone lifted AI confidence overnight — but note the rotation cost: institutions trimmed liquid mega-cap AI holdings on Wednesday to fund the allocation, which helped knock Nvidia −2.89% a day after its earnings beat. The debut's first trade, not the pricing, is the sentiment test. TradeTank66, Morningstar, Stocktwits
  2. The hawkish minutes are the counterweight to the IPO euphoria. "Almost all" FOMC officials expect further hikes, several flagged a December move larger than 25 bp, and Waller openly floated 50 bp. The minutes also flagged AI demand as a material inflation risk and rapid growth in AI-related debt through "complex and opaque financing structures." The market is pricing a December hike at ~90–92% (25 bp) with ~8–10% on 50 bp — the hike is priced, the size is not. A strong Anthropic debut against that backdrop is a sentiment trade, not a rates trade. Ultima Markets, Rivkin, The Retest
  3. Nvidia's beat is fading into a leadership story. The stock is −36.9% from its October 6 peak after Wednesday's −2.89%, with Bernstein and Evercore downgrading, Moody's cutting its outlook, and the succession search now approaching Arm CEO Rene Haas. The earnings beat ($115.8B revenue, $130B Q4 guide) bought one premarket bounce and one session — the tape is trading the CEO question and the AI-spending question now, not the quarter. That is why SOXL/SOXS, not TQQQ/SQQQ, remain the week's highest-volatility pair. Hindu Business Line, Rivkin
  4. Gold and metals broke down while stocks bounced — the hedge is not hedging. Gold fell −3.41% Wednesday to a 14-month low, silver −4.98% to $45.00, copper −5.30%, with the dollar at a 19-month high (DXY 103.78). Overnight gold touched $3,693 before stabilising near $3,725. With the 10-year pinned above 5% and a December hike ~90% priced, the rate trade is crowding out the safety trade — DUST, not NUGT, is the gold pair with momentum, and $3,693 is the level that decides whether the rout resumes. Ainance, NordFX
  5. Asia split overnight — Taiwan's −4.6% is the warning label. Taiwan's chip-heavy Taiex fell 4.60% while Korea (+1.39%) and Japan (+0.71%) rose and MSCI Asia ex-Japan hit a seven-month low. The AI complex is not moving as one tape overnight; the US premarket bounce is led by the Anthropic headline, not by a global risk-on move. NordFX, The Retest
