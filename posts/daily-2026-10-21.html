Sentiment Drivers

  1. The AI-credit unwind got its first strong counter-evidence (bullish). SpaceX (SPCX) reported 161% YoY Q3 revenue growth ($14.58B) and Elon Musk disclosed SpaceX and Tesla formed special committees to explore a potential merger — SPCX +8.6 to +10.5% premarket. Super Micro (SMCI) raised preliminary FQ1 sales and gross-margin guidance — +12% premarket, lifting Dell, HPE, CoreWeave and Nebius with it. Micron +2% on a Taichung labor deal. For a market that spent two weeks pricing AI distress (CoreWeave lows, record hedge-fund tech cutting), this is the first demand-side rebuttal. tipranks, fxstreet, invezz
  2. "Beats aren't enough" — Netflix −6% (bearish for the guide-sell tape). NFLX posted a narrow beat ($0.87 EPS vs $0.82; $12.87B revenue in line) but cut its 2026 ad revenue forecast to ~$2.7B from ~$3.0B, guided Q4 light ($13.44B vs $13.51B), and reported its slowest top-line growth in three years — its sixth straight post-earnings decline. Intuitive Surgical, Danaher and AT&T also beat and traded lower premarket. The market is punishing forward guidance, not the quarter. investingcube, samuelandco, particle
  3. Oil back over $110 vs. weak housing data — cross-currents. Brent +1.3% to ~$110.2 on US–Iran deal headlines; UK CPI came in hot at 3.5%. But September US housing data was bleak across the board — starts −5.2% to 1.221M annual pace, permits missed (expected +2%), pending sales −7.3% to a record low — which kept the 10-yr soft at 5.125% and the dollar down. Energy bulls vs. rate bulls cancel out at the index level; oil is a tax on the growth side of the barbell. talkmarkets, financialadvsr, samuelandco
  4. Rotation, not capitulation (neutral-to-bullish breadth). Dow +1.0% while Nasdaq finished flat, Russell +1.65%, NYSE advancers outnumbered decliners nearly 3:1 — defensive/cyclical money is active. FOMC blackout through Oct 27–28; FedWatch prices an 85% chance of a hold next week; Warsh says he doesn't "prejudge." financialadvsr, fxstreet
  5. Asia's chip rebound has a credibility test built in (neutral). KOSPI +3% and Elice's limit-up bounce say Korea is buying the dip in AI capex — but Elice's whipsaw from 400x-oversubscribed IPO to −32% to +30% in two days is retail volatility, not conviction. Japan's export strength (+16.7%) is real but oil-heavy imports are the offset.
