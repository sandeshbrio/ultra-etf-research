Sentiment Drivers

  1. The bounce is stabilization, not capitulation's end (neutral-to-cautious). Futures up <1% after Friday's worst session since June, the KOSPI's +2.24% rebound clawing back only a fraction of its −16.19% October, and the 10-year pinned near a seven-week low all say the same thing: Friday's stress is easing at the edges, not reversing. CNN Fear & Greed closed Friday at 26 (Fear); the weekly brief's retail Extreme Fear read (1) against it is the setup that historically precedes sharp relief rallies — but Tuesday's election and Friday's payrolls are two genuine binaries that can deepen Fear first. The Edge, Reuters
  2. Oil's slide is the morning's real easing (bullish for risk, bearish for energy longs). Brent −3 to −4.4% and WTI back below $100 on US–Iran diplomacy hopes remove the geopolitical tax that helped drive Friday's tape (Brent had closed Friday at $117.64). It eases the inflation leg of the December-hike case — but it also removes energy's bid, so don't look to oil names for cover today. TipRanks, AskTraders
  3. Apple tonight is the week's first single-stock verdict (the day's main event). Consensus ~$113B revenue (+~11% y/y), ~$1.99 EPS, iPhone revenue ~$56.5B (+15.3%), in the debut quarter under new CEO Tenus — and Apple has been the relative calm in the AI-spending storm, having "skirted the hyperscaler frenzy," with shares up four straight months and recently retaking the world's-most-valuable title. A clean beat with a solid Q1 guide (~$152.6B expected) is the Nasdaq's first genuine stabilization catalyst since Alphabet's fall; a soft guide extends the unwind into Election Day. Options imply a ~4% post-earnings move. Reuters Morning Bid, CMC Markets, Devdiscourse/Reuters
  4. The AI-spending trial continues, but the grading curve may be shifting (watch, don't assume). Friday's verdict punished cash-burning spenders (Alphabet, Amazon, Intel); Apple's core-margins-and-buyback story is graded on a different curve, and Seoul's institutional dip-buying in Samsung/SK Hynix plus SOXX +2% premarket suggest some buyers are willing to re-engage at these levels. If Apple holds and AMD (Tue AMC) beats, the "AI is dead" trade unwinds fast — which is exactly why SOXS/SQQQ are extended, not safe. Reuters, AJP
  5. Election positioning starts today, but results are tomorrow night's gap risk (positioning day). Markets stay open Tuesday; results land through the evening and control of Congress may not be known for days. Monday is a positioning day — size for a headline-driven tape into the close, and carry nothing leveraged through Apple's print or election night. Reuters, TradingView
