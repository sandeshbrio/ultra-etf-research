Sentiment Drivers

  1. CoreWeave validated AI-infrastructure demand — the first genuine demand-side print since the rout. Q3 revenue $3.66B, +168% y/y, beating the $3.55B consensus; full-year 2026 guidance raised to $13.0–13.3B; management said the company expects to be profitable on an adjusted basis in H1 2028, easing debt-burden fears. Shares are +13–17% premarket, and the whole neocloud complex is following (Nebius +14.9%, Applied Digital +9.5%, IREN +7.9%). The FT reports CoreWeave also plans to raise billions in GPU-backed debt at 7–7.5% to fund hardware for an unnamed investment-grade client. This is the validation the week-9 brief said the chip tape needed — but it arrives as a +15% SOXL gap, priced in premarket, not as an intraday entry. Reuters via Hot96, AIStockWire, TradingKey
  2. Hot CPI locked in the December hike — and put a 50 bp move on the table. Core +0.3% for a third straight month is exactly Waller's 50 bp trigger, and FedWatch now shows a hike as a certainty with the 50 bp probability at ~11.5–13.6%. The 10-year is parked at 5% with the bond market closed today, so the rates tape cannot confirm or reject the equity bounce until Thursday. If yields push through 5% when Treasuries reopen, the chip bounce meets its headwind immediately. Reuters via SRN, QMM Capitals/CNBC
  3. Nvidia is bouncing (+3.7% premarket) but the probe overhang is unchanged. The +3.7% only retraces part of a −9.5% two-day slide, and the stock has shed ~$480B of value in two sessions as questions about Huang's relationship with a Chinese businesswoman alleged to be a spy intensified. The board says it has "no reason to believe" secrets were compromised; three House committee chairmen want a closed-door briefing with Huang on Nov 18, and Nvidia reports earnings Tue Nov 17. A single-name governance hit can still re-open the chip tape — SOXL's gap is built on names that include this one. Reuters via Hot96, Finnhub (NVDA)
  4. Oil is firming again and gold is cooling — the two switches flipped in opposite directions. Brent touched $93.61 intraday on renewed attacks on Gulf energy routes after collapsing ~6% Monday; a further rise re-adds the inflation pressure that Tuesday's CPI just confirmed, while gold's >2% drop says the haven trade is unwinding with the risk bounce. Watch whether oil's firmness survives the session — if it does, it undercuts the CPI-relief half of today's rally. Upstox, investingLive

Trading Lens

  • A +15% SOXL gap is a price, not an entry. CoreWeave's validation is real, but it is fully repriced premarket — the gap-fade/extension record since Oct 16 now runs 14 holds vs 5 fades, and this week's gaps have held (Monday's +22.25% SOXL session extended all day). The rule-8 confirmation check at ~10:30 AM is the discriminator: a gap that is still extending with the driver worsening (more AI names confirming, NVDA holding its bounce) is the continuation shape; a gap fading toward Tuesday's close by the check is the stand-aside. Do not chase the open.
  • Veterans Day tape is thin and unanchored — cut size or stand aside. No bond trading, no data, no Fed speakers: the session has no Treasury anchor and low volume, so a CPI aftershock or a CoreWeave reaction can run further than it should in either direction. The ledger's standing watch already flags this (F5: thin tapes). If a setup passes, size down.
  • The 5% 10-year is the unresolved question, not today's bounce. The bond market reopens Thursday with yields parked at the line and a 50 bp December hike now priced at ~12%. If Thursday's reopening pushes the 10-year through 5%, today's chip gap meets its headwind at the same time as jobless claims and five Fed speakers. Nothing leveraged carries into Thursday's reopening.
  • Friday's PPI is the week's second binary — stay flat into it, same as Tuesday. A hot PPI (+0.4% headline / +0.3% core expected) on top of a hot CPI would harden the 50 bp talk; a soft one is the only print that can cut December-hike odds. Between now and then, the book's job is to trade today's reaction, not to hold a view.
